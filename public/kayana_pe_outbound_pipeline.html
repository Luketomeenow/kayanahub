Trigger Events

Signals that trigger outreach

Not all signals are equal. The post-acquisition window is Kayana's highest-converting signal — the portco is in chaos, the PE firm is demanding execution, and no one has an ops team yet.

Critical 0–72 hrs
New PE acquisition announced
PE firm closes on a lower-middle-market company. The portco immediately needs ops infrastructure — usually before a 100-day plan even exists.
→ Trigger Tier A urgent sequence immediately
Critical 48 hrs
COO / VP Ops role posted at portco
A job posting for ops leadership at a PE-backed company signals a gap — either the role is vacant or they're scaling. Both are Kayana opportunities.
→ Trigger Tier A sequence to CEO / PE partner
High 1 week
Portco headcount 2× in 90 days
Rapid headcount growth means the ops layer is breaking. They need EAs, chiefs of staff, and systems builders — now.
→ Enroll in Tier B expansion sequence
High 72 hrs
PE firm closes new fund
A new fund close means active deal deployment is imminent. Build the PE partner relationship before they start acquiring — so Kayana is already top of mind at close.
→ Trigger PE partner warm intro sequence
High 1 week
New portco CEO or leadership install
PE firms often install new leadership post-acquisition. New CEOs want to build their own ops team — they're receptive to external solutions in month 1.
→ Enroll new leader in personalized Tier A flow
Medium 2 weeks
Portco announces expansion / new market
Geographic or product expansion creates ops complexity. The company needs remote execution capacity before headcount can catch up.
→ Enroll in Tier B growth sequence
The 48-Hour Rule
Research from PE advisory firms consistently shows that the first vendor to reach a portco operator post-acquisition has a 3–4× higher close rate than those who reach out 2+ weeks later. The operator is still in "what do I need?" mode — not "we've already figured it out." Kayana's entire competitive advantage in outbound is winning this window before anyone else.

Prioritization Logic
The system auto-scores accounts on: recency of acquisition signal (40%) + company size fit (30%) + decision-maker seniority (20%) + ops tool stack gap (10%). Accounts scoring 70+ get Tier A treatment. 40–70 get Tier B. Below 40 get Tier C nurture only.

Claude API Prompt Logic
Each template above is the output structure. The Claude API prompt instructs the model to fill all {{variables}} from enriched Clay data, match the tone to the persona tier, reference one specific recent signal (acquisition, job post, funding), and keep the email under 120 words. Three subject line variants are generated per email and the highest-performing variant (tracked over 90 days) is automatically selected.

What to Optimize First
If open rate is low → fix subject lines and domain reputation. If reply rate is low → fix the first email body (likely too long or not signal-specific enough). If positive reply → meeting conversion is low → fix the response handling speed (replies need a human response within 2 hours). The biggest lever in this entire system is speed of response to positive replies — that's the only human-in-the-loop moment that matters.
